Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Introduction

Quality Group ("we", "us", "our") operates PostPilot (postpilot.zypsapp.com). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal data when you use our Service. We are committed to protecting your privacy and complying with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDP Act), the Information Technology Act 2000, and other applicable Indian laws.

This policy should be read alongside our Terms of Service.

1. Data We Collect

1.1 Information you provide directly

  • Name, email address, mobile number during registration
  • Business name, address, GST number (optional)
  • Profile photo (optional)
  • Social media account credentials (OAuth tokens — encrypted, never stored as plaintext passwords)
  • Content you create or upload through the Service (posts, images, captions)
  • Payment information (we do not store any card or payment details; all payment processing is handled entirely by Cashfree Payments in India and by Stripe for international payments)
  • Support communications (emails, messages to our team)

1.2 Information collected automatically

  • IP address, browser type, operating system, device type
  • When you start a free trial: a device fingerprint — a one-way code (hash) calculated in your browser from its settings (screen, time zone, language, graphics and font characteristics). We never receive the settings themselves; the code is used only to stop the same free trial being claimed repeatedly, and is kept after an account is deleted for that purpose alone
  • Pages visited, features used, time spent on the Service
  • Error logs and performance data
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies (see Section 8)

1.3 Information from third-party platforms

  • When you connect a social media account, we receive profile information, page details, and access tokens from that platform
  • Analytics data from connected platforms (likes, comments, reach, impressions)
  • Google Business Profile data if connected
  • We receive only what each platform's API grants us based on the permissions you approve

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to:

  • Provide, operate, and improve the Service
  • Publish content to your connected social media accounts on your instructions
  • Retrieve analytics and inbox messages from connected platforms
  • Send transactional emails (subscription confirmation, payment receipts, expiry reminders)
  • Send service notifications (backup alerts, error alerts, post publishing confirmations)
  • Respond to your support requests
  • Detect and prevent fraud and unauthorised access
  • Comply with legal obligations under applicable Indian law
  • Improve our AI content suggestions (using anonymised, aggregated data only — never your specific content or personal information)

We do NOT:

  • Sell your personal data to any third party
  • Use your content to train AI models without your explicit consent
  • Share your data with third parties for their marketing purposes
  • Use your data for any purpose not described in this policy

Legal bases for processing: contractual necessity (to provide the Service), legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, service improvement), consent (marketing communications, optional features), and legal obligation (compliance with Indian law).

3. Data Sharing

We share your data only with:

3.1 Service providers who help us deliver the Service

  • Supabase (database hosting — servers in the EU/US)
  • Cloudflare R2 (media file storage)
  • Resend (transactional email delivery)
  • Cashfree Payments (payment processing — licensed by RBI)
  • Stripe (payment processing for international transactions)
  • Google Cloud (AI content generation via Gemini API)
  • Anthropic (AI fallback via Claude API)
  • Pollinations.ai (AI image generation)
  • Kling AI (AI video generation)
  • Brave Search (web mention monitoring)
  • Render (application hosting)
  • Sentry (error monitoring — anonymised error data only)
  • Backblaze (encrypted backup storage)

All service providers are bound by data processing agreements and may only use your data to provide their services to us.

3.2 Social media platforms

When you instruct us to publish content or retrieve data, we share the necessary information with the relevant platform (Meta, Google, LinkedIn, X/Twitter, TikTok, Pinterest, Telegram, Threads).

3.3 Legal requirements

We may disclose your data to comply with a court order, legal obligation, or request from a competent Indian government authority. We will notify you of such requests where legally permitted.

3.4 Business transfers

If Quality Group is acquired or merged, your data may be transferred to the acquiring entity, which will be required to honour this Privacy Policy. We will notify you by email before such a transfer.

We do not share your data with any other third parties.

4. International Data Transfers

Some of our service providers (Supabase, Render, Backblaze, Google, Anthropic) process data outside India. Where this occurs, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place including contractual protections. By using the Service, you consent to your data being processed in the countries where our service providers operate, subject to the protections described in this policy.

5. Data Retention

  • Account and profile data: retained while your account is active and for 90 days after deletion (to allow recovery if deletion was accidental)
  • Published post data and analytics: retained for the duration of your subscription plus 90 days
  • Payment records: retained for 7 years as required by Indian tax law
  • Support communications: retained for 2 years
  • Error logs: retained for 90 days
  • Encrypted backups: retained for 90 days on backup servers

After these periods, data is permanently deleted or anonymised. You may request earlier deletion (see Section 7).

6. Data Security

We protect your data using:

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS for all connections)
  • Encryption at rest for backups (AES-256)
  • OAuth tokens stored encrypted in our database
  • Access controls limiting who on our team can access your data
  • Regular automated backups to two independent offsite locations
  • Sentry error monitoring to detect anomalies
  • Regular security reviews

We will notify you within 72 hours of becoming aware of a data breach that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and interests, as required under applicable Indian law.

7. Your Rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Data portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format (JSON or CSV)
  • Withdrawal of consent: Withdraw consent for processing at any time, without affecting prior processing
  • Grievance redressal: File a complaint with our Data Protection Officer or with the Data Protection Board of India

To exercise any of these rights, email us at support@zypsapp.com with the subject "Privacy Request — [Your Request Type]". We will respond within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity before processing requests.

8. Cookies

We use session cookies for login only (essential, cannot be disabled). We do not currently use analytics or advertising cookies.

9. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If we become aware that we have collected data from a minor, we will delete it promptly. If you believe we have collected data from a minor, please contact us at support@zypsapp.com.

10. Links to Third-Party Sites

The Service may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by email at least 14 days before they take effect. The current version is always available at postpilot.zypsapp.com/privacy. Continued use of the Service after changes take effect constitutes acceptance.
