Nothing to download

PostPilot posts for you from our server. There is no app to install and no computer that has to stay on. Getting started takes about 3 minutes.

  1. Connect your accounts. Open the Connect My Accounts button in your welcome email (or WhatsApp) and sign in to Facebook, Instagram and the others, one tap each.
  2. Open your account. Log in here with your license key (or an email link). It remembers you on that device, so you only do it once.
  3. That is it. PostPilot writes and posts for you. Your account page shows your results, your plan and your invoices.

Prefer to post from your own computer? (optional, more technical)

The desktop app keeps your posts, queue and history on your own computer (Windows, Mac or Linux). Setup takes about 20 minutes, and the computer must be on at your posting times.

Download the desktop app
  1. Download from the link above. On Windows, choose ZypsPostPilotSetup.exe. On Mac or Linux, choose the .zip and unzip it (it needs Python 3.11 or newer).
  2. Run the setup wizard (ZypsPostPilotSetup.exe, or python setup_wizard.py in the unzipped folder) and paste your license key and signup email.
  3. Start PostPilot with start.bat (Windows) or start.sh (Mac/Linux). Your dashboard opens at localhost:8080/dashboard.
  4. New computer, or lost your key? Install again and paste your key in the wizard, or WhatsApp us and we will help you recover it.

Windows may warn about an unrecognised app the first time. Choose More info → Run anyway.
