How to Delete Your Data

Last updated: October 2026

PostPilot is committed to your right to data deletion under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and applicable Indian law. This page explains how to request deletion of your data.

1. Delete your PostPilot account

To delete your PostPilot account and all associated personal data:

  1. Email support@zypsapp.com with subject line: "Delete My Account — [Your Registered Email]"
  2. Include your registered email address and business name
  3. We will verify your identity and confirm your deletion request within 48 hours
  4. Your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted within 30 days
  5. You will receive a confirmation email once deletion is complete

What gets deleted

  • Your PostPilot account and profile information
  • All scheduled and published post records
  • Connected social media account tokens
  • Analytics and insights data
  • Team member records associated with your account
  • All personal information provided during signup

What is retained (as required by law)

  • Payment transaction records (7 years, as required by Indian tax law)
  • Records required to comply with legal obligations or resolve disputes

2. Remove PostPilot's access to your Facebook or Instagram

If you connected your Facebook or Instagram account to PostPilot and wish to revoke access:

Method 1 — Through Facebook Settings

  1. Go to facebook.com → Settings & Privacy → Settings
  2. Click "Apps and Websites" in the left menu
  3. Find "Zyps PostPilot" in the list
  4. Click "Remove"
  5. This immediately revokes PostPilot's access to your Facebook and Instagram data

Method 2 — Request data deletion

Email support@zypsapp.com with subject: "Delete My Facebook/Instagram Data — [Your Registered Email]"

We will delete all data associated with your Facebook/Instagram connection within 30 days and confirm by email.

3. Remove PostPilot's access to your Google account

  1. Go to myaccount.google.com/permissions
  2. Find "Zyps PostPilot" in the list of apps with access
  3. Click on it and select "Remove Access"
  4. This immediately revokes PostPilot's access to your Google Business Profile and other Google services

4. Remove access to other platforms

For LinkedIn, X/Twitter, TikTok, Pinterest, Telegram, and Threads — you can revoke PostPilot's access directly from each platform's settings, or email support@zypsapp.com and we will revoke access and delete associated tokens within 30 days.

5. Data portability (export before deletion)

Before deleting your account, you may request a copy of your data in JSON or CSV format. Email support@zypsapp.com with subject: "Data Export Request — [Your Registered Email]". We will provide your data export within 30 days.
